    Research Intern

    Summer of 2023 | Penn State University

    I researched 2-Dimensional Semiconductors with Dr. Saptarshi Das's research team under the mentorship of Rahul Pendurthi and Darsith Jayachandran.

    We were researching how ammonia annealing would affect 2D MOSFETs in collaboration with Dr. Joan Redwing's team.

    The Redwing team grew and treated (ammonia annealed) the 2D Semiconductor films on a sapphire substrate.

    We (in the Das team) took those grown films and transfered them onto silica substrates and developed them into 2D MOSFETs.

    We found that the ammonia annealing damaged the 2D film, as very few of the treated transistors functioned, and those that did functioned poorly.

    While the research itself was unsuccessful, I was able to get a better grasp on nanoscale devices and VLSI fundamentals, as well as using exotic materials with state-of-the-art machinery.

    I was trained on and independently prepared and operated machinery for:

    • Optical Lithography
    • E-Beam Evaporation
    • Dry and Wet Spin Benches
    • Characterization of nanoscale devices

    I also shadowed and assisted my mentors in:

    • E-beam lithography
    • RIE (Reactive-Ion Etching)
